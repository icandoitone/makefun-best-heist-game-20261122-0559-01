**The Gilded Vault** — a top-down museum heist. Mara Voss, the Velvet Fox, has one night in the Aurelia Museum: lift the Star of Aurelia sapphire from the Grand Gallery, crack the Duchess vault safe in the Vault Wing, and slip out over the skylight rope to the getaway. Three wings, patrolling guards with vision cones, sweeping wall cameras, sliding laser grids, loot tiers from pocket coins to the crown, coin tosses to lure guards, smoke pellets to hide, and one shared alarm meter — let it peak and the night is over.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No network, no build step: `game/` is plain static files. Move with WASD or arrows, Shift to sneak, E to lift loot and crack the safe (Space stops the dial on the green arc), click to throw a coin, F for a smoke pellet, M to mute, R to restart the wing.
